LG Bag Lock

Adds a lock button next to the default bag sort button. Click the lock, then click an item to lock it to its slot (blue border). Click the lock and the item again to unlock it. Locks are per character and follow the item if you move it manually.

The sort button runs the addon's own sort and never moves locked items. Order: Hearthstone, food, weapons, armor, crafting items, quest items, everything else, each by quality (best on the left). Junk fills from the last slot backwards. Specialty and reagent bags are filled first.

Install: copy the LGBagLock folder to Interface\AddOns.
